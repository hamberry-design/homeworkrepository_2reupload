Cubchoo

Type: Ice

Cubchoo is a small polar bear themed Pokemon. Coming in at 18.7 pounds, it may be small but can cause mass destruction. It is an ice type and is weak to fire, rock, fighting, and steel. If you give Cubchoo enough time to prove itself, you'll be met with a Beartic. Beware Beartic.
